# FP&A in Excel: Budgeting, Forecasting & Variance Analysis

**Course:** FP&A with Excel, PW Skills
**Author:** Ajinkya Nandanpawar, MSc Financial Management (VU Amsterdam), former Financial Analyst

## Summary

As part of the PW Skills FP&A course, I built a set of Excel models covering the core of the FP&A cycle: planning (budgeting), looking ahead (forecasting and scenario analysis), checking performance (variance analysis) and communicating results (data visualization). Together, they show how a finance team moves from plan to actuals to decisions.

## What I Built

### 1. Budgeting Model
- Built an operating budget with revenue and cost assumptions kept on separate input cells
- Structured the workbook so changing an assumption updates the whole budget automatically
- Produced a period-by-period budget view suitable for management review

### 2. Forecasting
- Built forecasts on top of the budget and historical figures
- Kept forecast drivers (such as growth and cost assumptions) editable so the forecast can be updated as new information arrives

### 3. Scenario Analysis
- Modelled alternative cases (for example base, best and worst) to show how the outcome changes when key assumptions move
- Compared scenarios side by side to show the range of possible results and the risk around the plan

### 4. Variance Analysis
- Compared actuals against budget to calculate variances in both value and percentage
- Identified favourable and unfavourable variances to show where performance diverged from plan and where to investigate

### 5. Data Visualization & Reporting
- Turned the model outputs into charts and summary views
- Presented budget, forecast and variance results in a form that a non-finance manager can read quickly

## Skills Demonstrated

- Budgeting and forecasting
- Scenario and sensitivity thinking
- Variance analysis and performance reporting
- Financial modelling structure: inputs, calculations and outputs kept separate
- Excel formulas, charts and dashboards
- Turning numbers into a clear message for decision-makers
- Providing actionable insights to the stakeholders and management based on the analysis

## Why It Matters

These models mirror the work of an FP&A or commercial finance analyst: owning the budget, keeping the forecast current, explaining variances to the business and supporting decisions with scenarios.
